01

Vision & Positioning

SportsJack turns every quarter of an NFL game into a two-minute hand of blackjack — except the cards are real players, and their values swing live with the broadcast.

The one-liner: Hit or stand on a hand of NFL players, racing to 21 fantasy points while the game is still being played. The closer the kickoff-to-whistle drama maps onto "do I take another card?", the more this wins.

Why blackjack, not poker

Blackjack's single decision — take another card or hold — is the cleanest possible mapping to live sports tension. "This receiver is heating up, I'll take him" or "I'm sitting on 19, don't blow it" needs zero poker literacy. It is glanceable, fast, and universally understood, which matters enormously for a mobile, crypto-onboarding audience.

Who it's for

  • Primary: 21–40, sports-watching, mobile-first, already bets or plays DFS, crypto-curious to crypto-native.
  • Secondary: Casual blackjack / casino-app players who want a reason to care about a game they have no team in.

02

Design Pillars & Principles

Every feature decision should ladder up to one of these four pillars. If it doesn't, cut it from MVP.

⚡Live or it's dead

The product's whole reason to exist is that values move while you watch. Latency, smooth number animation, and "feed is live" trust are P0, not polish.

👁Glanceable under pressure

A decision must be makeable in 3 seconds with one thumb while watching a TV. One hero number (distance to 21), one risk read, two buttons.

🎰Vegas feel, fair core

Tactile chips, dealing animations, win shimmer, haptics — the dopamine of a casino — riding on a provably-fair, auditable engine.

🪙Crypto without the friction

"Yards" abstracts USDC so the unit feels native to football, not finance. The wallet should disappear behind the game.

◆ Design proposal — naming

Currency = Yards (1 YD = 1 USDC). It's sport-native, reads as "ground gained," and sidesteps casino/finance baggage. Reinforce with copy: "gained 1,080 yards," "yards on the table," "cash out your yards." Power-ups and cosmetics priced in yards too.

03

Game Design — The Core Loop

One NFL quarter = one hand. A game gives you four hands; a Sunday slate gives you dozens.

Ante & deal (kickoff)

Player posts an ante in yards to join the quarter's hand and is dealt an opening hand of NFL players (proposed: 2 cards). Each card's value = that player's live fantasy points, scored continuously.

Watch the values move

As the real game plays, card values rise (yards, catches, TDs) and can fall (fumbles lost, sacks, INTs → negative plays). Your hand total drifts toward — or past — 21 on its own.

Hit or stand, on demand

At any moment you may Hit (draw another player into your hand, raising your ceiling and your bust risk) or Stand (stop drawing). Power-ups — Freeze, Burn — can be spent to bend variance.

The bust window is alive

Unlike casino blackjack, going over 21 is not instant death: a player on your hand can post negative plays and pull your total back under 21. Busting is a live, recoverable state until the quarter ends.

Whistle & settle (end of quarter)

At 0:00 the quarter's stats freeze. Hands are scored: closest to 21 without finishing over wins. Yards settle from escrow; rake is taken; standings update.

Re-buy or ride

Player chooses to play the next quarter (same or new table), cash out, or hop to another live game. Loop repeats Q1→Q4 and across the slate.

▲ Decision needed — "stand" semantics

Does Stand freeze your total, or just stop you drawing more cards while your existing cards keep moving live? These are very different games. Recommendation: Stand = stop drawing, but cards stay live (true to the "live deck" fantasy). Locking your number is exactly what the Freeze power-up is for — that separation keeps each action meaningful.

04

Mechanics Deep Dive

The fun is in the details — and several of these are still genuinely undefined. Flagged inline.

4.1 Scoring — what a card is worth

A card's face value is its live fantasy score, computed from a published scoring table (PPR-style). This must be a single, frozen-per-season ruleset shown to users.

EventProposed pointsNotes
Passing yards0.04 / yd (1 per 25)QB-heavy; tune to keep totals near the 21 band
Rushing / receiving yards0.1 / yd
Reception (PPR)+1.0Half-PPR is an option to flatten variance
Touchdown+6.0A single TD is a huge swing near 21 — intentional drama
Turnover (INT / fumble lost)−2.0 to −4.0Critical: negatives are what make bust recoverable
Sack taken (QB)−1.0
● Gap — score scaling to the 21 target

A real NFL quarter rarely produces ~21 fantasy points for two players. We must define a scaling / normalization so a typical strong hand lands in the 17–21 zone — otherwise nobody ever approaches 21 and the blackjack metaphor breaks. Options: (a) scale all stats by a season-tuned multiplier, (b) use per-quarter projections, (c) redefine the target (e.g. "race to a moving par"). This is the single most important unresolved mechanic.

4.2 The deck — where cards come from

Undefined today. Core questions the engine depends on:

  • Deck composition: Is the "deck" the full set of active players in this game (≈90 players), the two starting offenses, or a curated pool of skill-position starters?
  • Shared vs personal: Can two players hold the same NFL player as a card simultaneously? (Proposed: yes — players aren't consumed; everyone draws from the same conceptual pool. Simpler, and avoids supply starvation at full tables.)
  • Draw randomness: Weighted by projection, or flat-random? Weighted draws make Hit less of a coin-flip and reward sports knowledge.
  • Position rules: Any constraints (max one QB? must be from the live game?) or fully open?
▲ Decision needed — deck model

Recommendation: Per-game shared pool of skill-position starters + key backups (~20–24 players), non-consuming, weighted-random draws. Small enough to learn, deep enough for variety, and "from the game you're watching" keeps every card meaningful on the broadcast.

4.3 Hit mechanics

  • Does Hitting cost yards (an additional ante per card), or is it free as in casino blackjack? decision
  • Is there a maximum hand size? (Proposed cap: 5 cards — keeps the UI legible and the math bounded.)
  • Can you Hit after you Stand? (Proposed: no — Stand is a commitment.)
  • Is a drawn card's entry value its value at draw time or does it join live? (Proposed: joins live, like all cards.)

4.4 Bust, recovery & ties

  • Live bust: Over 21 is a warning state, not elimination — you can drift back under. The UI must make "you're busted right now, but it's recoverable" legible (we show a red gauge + "X over").
  • Final bust: If you finish the quarter over 21, how are you ranked? (Proposed: all non-busts beat all busts; among busts, closest to 21 from above ranks higher.)
  • Push / tie: Two players land identical totals — split the pot, or tie-break by fewer cards / earlier stand? decision
  • Everyone busts: Pot carries to next quarter, returns antes minus rake, or closest-from-above takes it? decision

4.5 Game modes

vs Players (PvP ring)

6-max table, shared pot of antes, closest to 21 wins, house takes rake. The flagship social mode. Needs liquidity to fill tables.

vs Dealer (house)

You vs an automated dealer hand. Always available, no liquidity dependency — critical for off-peak and onboarding. Needs a defined dealer strategy.

● Gap — dealer AI rules

Casino blackjack dealers follow a fixed rule ("hit until 17"). Our dealer faces a live, moving hand. We must define: when does the dealer Hit/Stand? Does it react to the live game or follow a deterministic schedule? Does it get power-ups? The house edge math and provable fairness both depend on this being a published, deterministic policy.

05

Economy, Yards & Wagering

Yards are the unit of everything: stakes, payouts, power-ups, cosmetics. 1 Yard = 1 USDC, held in escrow per hand.

5.1 The wager model

Today the prototype shows an "ante to sit." We need to define the full wagering surface:

ElementProposed MVPStatus
AnteFixed buy-in per quarter to be dealt inDefined
Raising mid-handShould you be able to add yards as your hand strengthens? Adds poker-like betting tension.decision
Double downBlackjack staple — double your wager, commit to your hand. Natural fit.decision
Side bets"Will any card hit a TD this drive?" — future revenue, post-MVPPhase 2
Min / max table stakesTiered tables (e.g. 25 / 100 / 1K yard rooms)Defined

5.2 Rake & house edge

  • PvP: 8–12% rake on the winning pot (tiered by stake; lower rake at high stakes).
  • vs Dealer: house edge baked into dealer policy + payout ratio rather than rake.
  • Rake must be shown transparently pre-hand (already surfaced on the buy-in screen).

5.3 Deposit, withdraw, custody

On-ramp

Connect wallet (Phantom, MetaMask, or in-app embedded wallet) → deposit USDC → credited as yards. Phase 2: fiat on-ramp (Apple Pay / card → USDC) to remove the crypto barrier.

Escrow

Antes lock into a per-hand escrow smart contract at deal. Nobody — including the house — can touch mid-hand.

Settlement

At whistle, the contract pays the winner, deducts rake, releases the rest. Instant Solana finality; yards balance updates in-app.

Off-ramp

Withdraw yards → USDC to wallet, any time, subject to no open hands. KYC gate above thresholds.

● Gap — wallet onboarding & custody UX

None of the connect-wallet, deposit, KYC, or withdraw screens are designed yet. This is the highest-friction part of the funnel and likely where most users churn. Needs: embedded-wallet option for non-crypto users, gas abstraction (users shouldn't think about SOL for fees), clear pending/confirmed states, and a recovery story for lost keys.

06

Power-Up System

Power-ups are the signature twist that separates SportsJack from "blackjack with a sports skin." They add a layer of resource management and read-the-game skill.

❄Freeze

Lock your hand's current total. Immune to further live movement — you bank the number you have right now, even if your players later fumble. The defensive play.

🔥Burn

Discard one card from your hand and draw a replacement. Bail out of a player having a disaster game, or gamble for a better ceiling. The aggressive play.

6.1 Proposed additional power-ups (future)

Power-upEffectArchetype
PeekSee the live value of the next card before you HitInformation
StealCopy an opponent's card into your hand (PvP only)Aggression
InsuranceRecover half your ante if you finish bustedHedge
SwapTrade one of your cards for a random unheld oneVariance

6.2 The power-up economy — undefined

● Gap — how do players get power-ups?

The single biggest open game-design question after scoring. Options, each with very different monetization & fairness implications:

  • Free per hand — everyone gets 1 Freeze + 1 Burn per quarter. Pure skill, no pay-to-win. (Current prototype assumption.)
  • Purchasable with yards — buy power-ups from the store. Revenue, but risks pay-to-win perception in real-money PvP.
  • Earned / loadout — unlock via play, equip a limited loadout pre-hand (battle-pass style progression).
  • Hybrid — 1 free per hand, extras purchasable only in vs-Dealer / play-money modes.

Recommendation: Free baseline in real-money PvP (protects fairness + regulatory posture); sell cosmetic power-up skins and extra charges only in casual modes.

⚠ Risk — pay-to-win in a real-money game

Selling power-ups that affect outcomes in real-money PvP invites both player backlash and regulatory scrutiny (it blurs "game of skill" defenses). Keep the competitive core power-up-symmetric.

08

Key User Journeys

Three journeys the app must nail. Each exposes missing screens above.

8.1 First-time user (cold install, Sunday morning)

Install → instant hook

No wall. Drop straight into a play-money vs-Dealer hand on a live game so they feel the mechanic in under 30 seconds. Teach Hit/Stand/Freeze/Burn via guided overlay.

"Want to play for real?"

After the demo hand, soft-prompt account creation. Explain yards = USDC in one sentence with a football metaphor.

Fund

Embedded wallet by default (no extension needed); deposit via fiat on-ramp or existing wallet. Gas abstracted. Minimal KYC until withdrawal threshold.

First real hand

Steer to a low-stakes table or vs-Dealer. Celebrate the first cash-out moment hard.

▲ Decision needed — gating

How much can a user do before account / wallet creation? Recommendation: unlimited play-money, zero-friction; gate only real-money. Maximizes top-of-funnel and softens the crypto barrier.

8.2 Returning user (game is live now)

Push notification

"RED–BLU kicks off in 5 min — 3 tables open." Deep-link straight to the lobby for that game.

Sit + ante in two taps

Remember last stake. One-tap "sit at my usual."

Play the quarter, settle, decide

Stay for next quarter, jump games, or cash out. Cross-game "what else is live" rail.

8.3 In-hand micro-loop (the 2 minutes that matter)

Glance

One look tells you: my total, distance to 21, bust risk, who's hot on my hand.

React to a play

A TD just swung your card +6 — do you Freeze to bank it, or ride? Live, emotional, fast.

Commit

Hit, Stand, or spend a power-up. Haptic + animation confirms. Back to watching the game.

09

Gap Analysis — What We Haven't Designed

Thinking like a Vegas game designer: here is the full inventory of workflows, mechanics, and edge cases not yet addressed. Grouped by severity.

9.1 Blocking — the game can't ship without these

🎯 Score normalization to 21

Without tuning real fantasy points into the 17–21 band, the blackjack metaphor collapses. Needs simulation against historical box scores.

🃏 Deck & draw rules

Composition, shared vs personal, weighting, position limits. The engine can't be built without this.

🤖 Dealer policy

Deterministic hit/stand logic for the house hand in vs-Dealer. Drives the whole non-PvP mode + house math.

🔌 Disconnection handling

Phone locks, signal drops mid-quarter. Auto-stand on your current hand? Auto-freeze? Money is live — this must be bulletproof and fair.

📡 Feed-loss fallback

If the stat feed dies mid-hand, do we pause, void & refund, or switch to a backup feed? Affects trust and settlement.

💳 Wallet / deposit / KYC flow

The entire money on-ramp is undesigned and is the #1 churn risk.

9.2 Important — needed for a good experience

  • Matchmaking & liquidity: How do tables fill? Do we seed with house bots early to avoid empty rooms? How are 6-max seats allocated across many concurrent games?
  • Late-join / sit-out: Can you join a quarter already in progress? Skip a quarter without leaving the table?
  • Tutorial & education: "Cards are players, values are live" is non-obvious. Needs an interactive first-run, plus a persistent "how to play."
  • Card detail view: Tap a card → live stat line, what's driving its value, game situation. Rewards the engaged user.
  • Notifications system: Kickoff alerts, "your hand is busting," quarter-result, re-engagement. Mobile lives on push.
  • Profile, stats & progression: Record, streaks, best stand, achievements — the retention spine of any casino app.
  • Responsible-play surface: Deposit/loss/time limits, cool-off, self-exclusion. Both ethical and regulatory.
  • Multi-table / multi-game: Power users will want to play several live games at once — how does the UI support watching many hands?

9.3 Sports edge cases — the messy real world

● Gap — live-sports reality

Real games break clean rules. We need defined behavior for each:

  • Player injured / benched mid-quarter — their card stops accruing. Auto-offer a free Burn? Freeze its value?
  • Stat corrections — the NFL revises stats post-game (a catch reclassified). Do settled hands ever re-settle? (Recommend: no — settle on the live feed at the whistle, final.)
  • Overtime / shortened quarters — does the quarter clock map cleanly? Weather/lightning delays?
  • Blowouts & garbage time — starters pulled; values flatline. Affects engagement late in lopsided games.
  • Game postponed / cancelled — void open hands, return antes.
  • Defensive / special-teams scoring — are DEF and K valid cards? They behave very differently from skill positions.

9.4 Social & retention (Phase 2, but design now)

  • Table chat / emotes / reactions — the casino floor feeling.
  • Friends, private tables, invites — viral loop.
  • Leaderboards & seasons — competitive ladder tied to the NFL calendar.
  • Daily streaks, missions, login rewards — habit formation.
  • Spectate mode — watch a friend's hand, watch high-stakes tables.

Settling every quarter on-chain is clean but costly/latent at scale. Alternative: off-chain balance ledger with periodic on-chain settlement & withdrawal. Recommendation: off-chain yards ledger for gameplay speed; on-chain only at deposit/withdraw and as a periodic provable checkpoint.

11

Real-Time Stat Engine

This is the heartbeat. If values lag, jump, or disagree with the broadcast, trust evaporates.

  • Latency target: a stat event (catch, TD) reflected in card values within <5s of the official feed, ideally tracking the broadcast.
  • Smoothing: values animate smoothly between updates; never teleport. Show direction (▲▼) and a subtle live pulse.
  • Single source of truth: the server's scored value is authoritative; clients only render.
  • Dual feed + reconciliation: primary and backup providers; flag & reconcile disagreements; auto-failover.
  • Feed-loss protocol: defined, visible "live data paused" state; rules for pause vs void; never silently settle on stale data.
  • Replay & audit: every value change logged with timestamp + source event, so any hand can be reconstructed for disputes.
⚠ Risk — feed reliability is existential

The entire product depends on a trustworthy live feed. A wrong or laggy value during a real-money hand is a settlement dispute and a trust break. Dual feeds, transparent pause states, and an audit log are non-negotiable P0.

12

Fairness & Anti-Cheat

Real money + randomness + multiplayer = a target. Fairness must be provable, not promised.

  • Provably-fair draws: card draws use a committed seed (commit-reveal / VRF) so players can verify no rigging.
  • Server-authoritative state: clients send intents (Hit/Stand/power-up), never outcomes. No client-trusted values.
  • Collusion detection: at shared tables, detect chip-dumping and coordinated play; flag anomalous patterns.
  • Bot / automation defense: rate limits, device attestation, behavioral signals.
  • Latency fairness: all players at a table see value updates on the same server tick — no edge from a faster connection.
  • Data-edge fairness: consider how a user watching a faster broadcast feed could gain an information edge over the app's feed, and whether a small action delay is needed.
13

Compliance & Responsible Play

Real-money skill gaming with crypto sits in a contested legal space. Design for compliance from day one, not as a bolt-on.

  • Jurisdiction & geofencing: launch only in permitted regions; geofence by IP + device; block restricted US states.
  • Age & identity: 18+ (or 21+ where required); KYC/AML via provider before real-money withdrawal.
  • Game-of-skill posture: the legal defensibility rests on skill dominating chance — power-ups, reads, and timing must demonstrably matter. Avoid mechanics that look like pure chance or pay-to-win.
  • Responsible gaming: deposit / loss / time limits, session reminders, cool-off, self-exclusion, and clear "this is real money" framing. Surface in Settings + onboarding.
  • Transparency: published scoring rules, rake, odds, and provably-fair verification.
  • AML / transaction monitoring: crypto in/out flows monitored; thresholds reported per regulation.
⚠ Risk — regulatory is the company-killer

Engage gaming-compliance counsel before build, not at launch. The MVP scope (which states, KYC thresholds, skill framing) should be drawn directly from that legal review. This gates everything.

Roadmap & Phasing

Phase 0 — Design lock (now)

Resolve the blocking game-design decisions (R1–R3), legal review, finalize mechanics. No engine work until scoring & deck are locked.

Phase 1 — Closed alpha (play-money)

Full loop on real live games with play-money yards. Validate the mechanic, tune normalization against real box scores, prove the feed. No crypto risk yet.

Phase 2 — Real-money MVP

Wallet, escrow, KYC, compliance, vs-Dealer + low-stakes PvP. Soft launch in 1–2 permitted jurisdictions. Native iOS/Android.

Phase 3 — Growth

Leaderboards, seasons, cosmetics, social, fiat on-ramp, more stakes & tables, additional sports.

◆ Recommendation — de-risk with a play-money alpha

The novel, unproven thing is the mechanic (does score-to-21 feel good on real games?), not the crypto. Prove that with play-money first; layer real-money settlement on a validated loop. It also generates the historical data needed to tune R1.
